KiCad official `kicad-packages3d` does not ship RK09K. The FreeCAD model origin is the body center with pins along X; the KiCad footprint origin is pad 1 with pins along Y. Footprint 3D transform: **rotate Z 90°**, **offset (7, −2.5, 0) mm** so the 2.5 mm pin row lands on pads 1–3.
